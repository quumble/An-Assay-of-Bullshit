# Review package

Four reviews of *From Fingerprints to Boundary Conditions: Model-Associated Style Under Distribution Shift*.

The primary assistant and three separately tasked subagents read the complete supplied draft. The subagents wrote independently before the primary assistant read their reviews. These are different analytical perspectives from AI reviewers, not independent human peer review. Agreement should not be treated as a calibrated confidence estimate.

| File | Reviewer perspective |
| --- | --- |
| `01_primary_review.md` | Overall contribution, inferential scope, and a practical revision sequence |
| `02_empirical_methods.md` | Experimental design, statistics, uncertainty, and measurement validity |
| `03_conceptual_rigor.md` | Definitions, counterexamples, identity resolution, and the logic of persistence |
| `04_structure_and_readability.md` | Developmental editing, reader experience, manuscript organization, and suggested wording |

## Main points of convergence

- The paper has a promising empirical and methodological contribution; the reported analyses warrant substantial revision rather than abandonment.
- Failed transfer limits the tested classifier's portability; it does not alone establish disappearance of model-associated information.
- Sample matching addresses one confound but does not make every environmental contrast directly comparable. Define the “animal” condition and supply exact prompts.
- Complete the missing tables, figures, references, and reproducibility details, and distinguish the meanings of the reported uncertainty intervals.

## Distinct contributions

The primary review prioritizes the shared-poetry-target comparison and model-version provenance. The empirical review details resampling, parser validation, truncation, and prior knowledge of May outcomes. The conceptual review distinguishes prediction, ordering, magnitude, and target separability, and examines provider-level versus individual-model recognition. The editorial review supplies a revised outline, a worked-example rewrite, and specific cuts.

All reviews concern the completed analyses as reported. No raw data or scripts were supplied, experiments were not replicated, and citations were not externally audited. Suggested additional analyses are recommendations, not work performed for this package. The source manuscript has not been changed.
