## Relationship to the May source-study reports

The May reports **The Average Bullshittings of Machines** (Study 1) and
**Six Ways to Write a Synthetic Poem** (Study 2) are antecedent reports of the source
studies, not these superseded September drafts. Preserve their bibliographic identity
and historical analyses. Their raw responses are reused in the September harmonized
secondary analysis; the September work does not constitute a new collection of model
responses or an independent-data replication of those reports.

The September manuscript family evaluates local endpoint recovery, transfer of a
source-trained attribution rule, and feature-rank stability across specified
production conditions. Later revisions add exploratory diagnostics and narrow some
earlier interpretations. In particular, pooled structural rank correlations must not
be read as unconditional structural instability, and the unfiltered near-chance
prose-to-poetry estimate is not the only source-inclusion sensitivity result.
